📖 Instant Setup Guide

How to configure your assigned DNS hostname on your devices.

🤖

Android (Private DNS)

  1. Open Settings ➔ Connections / Network.
  2. Tap Private DNS ➔ Select Private DNS provider hostname.
  3. Paste your assigned hostname and tap Save.
🍏

iPhone / iPad (iOS Profile)

  1. Tap the 🍏 iOS Profile button on your active pass above.
  2. Open Settings ➔ Profile Downloaded ➔ Tap Install.
  3. Enjoy system-wide encrypted TLS acceleration!
🪟

Windows / Mac (DoH)

  1. Open Chrome, Brave, or Edge ➔ Settings.
  2. Search Security / Use Secure DNS.
  3. Choose Custom and paste: https://<your-hostname>/dns-query.
